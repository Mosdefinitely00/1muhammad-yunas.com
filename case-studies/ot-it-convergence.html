Flagship Execution

From $0 to $40M ARR: Winning the OT/IT Convergence Market

The Challenge

I inherited an unstructured go-to-market motion for a highly capable Physical AI and Industrial IoT platform. The engineering depth was phenomenal, but the commercial traction was stagnant. The ICP was completely undefined, sales teams lacked Operational Technology (OT) fluency, and quota attainment hovered below 60%. The core issue: we were selling complex IT software features to OT plant managers who only cared about machine uptime.

The Execution Architecture

1. ICP Redefinition: We stopped trying to boil the ocean. I narrowed our focus exclusively to discrete manufacturing and upstream energy accounts with 500+ employees and active SCADA/DCS deployments. We mapped every deal to three precise stakeholders: the OT Sponsor, the IT Champion, and the CFO Economic Buyer.

2. Narrative Overhaul: I eliminated "IIoT platform" from our lexicon—it was too commoditized. We shifted the category narrative to "Physical Operations Intelligence," focusing entirely on translating predictive telemetry into quantifiable risk reduction and EBITDA preservation.

3. Sales Motion Redesign: Replaced open-ended tech trials with highly structured, 60-day Proof of Concept (POC) architectures tied directly to critical industrial safety and downtime benchmarks.

Verified Outcomes

By treating the sales force as our primary customer and aligning the technical narrative directly to CFO priorities, the commercial engine scaled rapidly, securing market dominance within 30 months.
